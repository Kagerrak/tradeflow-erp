## Proposed interface direction

- Personality: precise, dependable, and composed.
- Style: light-first industrial/editorial operations console, dense where the
  task benefits from density.
- Palette: warm paper neutrals, deep ink/navy, restrained safety orange, and
  semantic status colors.
- Avoid: neon cyan/purple, glassmorphism, gradients, excessive cards, and
  consumer-playful styling.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA, color-independent status, reduced motion,
  keyboard operation, and field-ready touch targets.
