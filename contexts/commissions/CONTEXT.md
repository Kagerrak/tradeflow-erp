# Commissions domain language

**Commission Plan**: An effective-dated set of eligibility, basis, rate, tier,
and reversal rules.

**Commission Participant**: A salesperson or other eligible payee assigned to a
commission plan.

**Commission Attribution**: The link between a qualifying sale and one or more
participants.

**Commission Basis**: The approved value used for calculation, such as net
sales, gross margin, invoiced value, or collected value.

**Commission Accrual**: A calculated amount earned under the plan but not yet
paid.

**Commission Adjustment**: An approved change with a reason and audit history.

**Commission Reversal**: A negative accrual caused by a return, credit,
cancellation, or payment reversal under the plan.

**Commission Statement**: A participant-facing projection of accruals,
adjustments, reversals, and payouts for a period.

**Commission Payout**: A recorded settlement of approved commission accruals.

The trigger for earning commission—delivery, invoicing, or customer
collection—is a configurable policy that must be selected before implementation.
