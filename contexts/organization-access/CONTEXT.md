# Organization and access domain language

**Company**: The single legal business entity operating TradeFlow in the first
release.

**Base Currency**: The immutable currency in which the Company's inventory
value and posted accounting values are expressed.

**Branch**: An operational unit of the Company that owns commercial documents
and scopes user access.

**User**: A person authorized to access TradeFlow. A User is not a Customer,
Contact, Supplier, or Commission Participant.

**Role**: A named collection of permissions assigned to a User.

**Capability**: A server-enforced permission to perform one business action.

**Role Template**: A configurable starting collection of Capabilities. Role
names do not themselves grant authority.

**Operational Scope**: The Branches and Warehouses within which a User may
perform an authorized action.

**Approval Authority**: A User's assigned Capability, Operational Scope, and
explicit financial or percentage limits for approving a business exception.

**Maker-Checker**: A control requiring an eligible approver other than the User
who requested the exception.
